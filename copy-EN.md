# 02 — THE RIO RUGBY WEEKEND

# THE TOURNAMENT YOU HAVE TO PLAY AT LEAST ONCE IN YOUR LIFE.

### But you’ll probably want to do it again.

A weekend packed with quality rugby and full days at Rio’s iconic beaches with some of your best friends — **and hundreds of new ones.**

Rio Beach Rugby is where competitive rugby meets summer energy, ocean views and the kind of experience that only Rio can offer.

### **Play rugby. Live Rio. Make it a weekend to remember.**

And there is no reason to fly all the way to Rio just for the weekend.

Come a few days early. Stay a few days longer. Explore the city, discover the food, see the sights, experience the culture — and turn the tournament into a proper rugby tour.

**Rio Beach Rugby happens just a couple of weeks before Carnival**, when summer is at its peak and the city is already starting to feel the energy building.

# 03 — THE TOURNAMENT

# HOWEVER YOU PLAY RUGBY, THERE’S A PLACE FOR YOU ON THE SAND.

Some teams come to Rio to win the whole thing.

Some come for the sun, the beach, their mates and a very good excuse to spend a few days in Brazil.

Most are somewhere in between.

### **All of them have a great weekend.**

Rio Beach Rugby brings together Men’s and Women’s teams of different levels from Brazil and around the world for two days of fast, fun and seriously enjoyable Beach Fives.

There will be good rugby.

There will also be plenty of laughing, sand, tired legs and stories for the flight home.

## BEACH FIVES. RIO STYLE.

### 5 A SIDE
More space. More running.

### NO RUCKS, NO MAULS
Keep the ball alive and moving.

### NO KICKING
Ball in hand. That’s the fun part anyway.

### NO SET PIECE FORMATIONS
More rugby.

### UNLIMITED SUBSTITUTIONS
Fresh legs — and plenty of chances to get the whole squad involved.

### MEN’S + WOMEN’S
Different competitions. Same beach. Same weekend.

# 04 — SINCE 2006

# WE’VE BEEN DOING THIS SINCE 2006.

What started as a tournament between friends has grown into the **biggest beach rugby event in the Americas**.

And somehow, the idea is still pretty simple:

### **Bring good people together. Play rugby. Enjoy Rio.**

Over the years, teams have made the trip from:

**BRAZIL · ARGENTINA · URUGUAY · CHILE · GERMANY · ITALY · JAPAN · PORTUGAL · NEW ZEALAND**
