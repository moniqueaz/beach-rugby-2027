# RIO BEACH RUGBY 2027
## Visual Identity + Web Art Direction System — v1.1

**Status:** Source of truth for the Rio Beach Rugby 2027 website and campaign design  
**Primary use:** ChatGPT, Claude, Canvas/design tools, developers, designers, copywriters  
**Event:** 23–24 January 2027 — Rio de Janeiro, Brazil  
**Organizer:** Rio Rugby Football Club  
**Primary conversion goal:** Attract men's and women's rugby teams from Brazil and abroad  
**Primary conversion channels:** WhatsApp and Instagram  
**Languages:** English + Portuguese first; Spanish next  
**Logo:** Use the adapted Rio Beach Rugby **2027 logo**. Do not redesign it.

---

# 0. SOURCE-OF-TRUTH RULE

When any older 2026 material conflicts with this document, **this document wins**.

The 2027 website is not a spectator-information portal and not a ticketing site. It is a **high-impact international recruitment and brand site for rugby teams**, using Rio de Janeiro as the emotional hook and quality beach rugby as the reason to travel.

The desired visitor reaction is:

> “I want to bring my team to Rio for this.”

---

# 1. BRAND POSITIONING

## 1.1 Core proposition

**Rio is the destination. Rugby is the perfect excuse.**

Rio Beach Rugby 2027 should feel like the ultimate rugby team weekend in Rio:
- quality rugby;
- beach;
- summer;
- friends;
- travel;
- competition;
- celebration;
- a distinctly Carioca atmosphere.

The tournament is both:
- **serious, competitive rugby**, and
- **a festive beach-rugby experience**.

Do not position it as a party with rugby attached.  
Do not position it as a sterile elite sports competition.

The balance is:

**RIO SUMMER / LIFESTYLE = dominant**  
**SPORT / PERFORMANCE = secondary proof point**  
**FESTIVE ENERGY = supporting layer**

# 6. WEBSITE ARCHITECTURE

## 01 — HERO / RIO BEACH RUGBY 2027
Purpose: immediate desire + event recognition.

Must include:
- 2027 logo;
- “Sun, Sea, Sand & Sidesteps”;
- 23–24 January 2027;
- Rio de Janeiro, Brazil;
- dominant Rio image;
- primary CTA: Bring Your Team to Rio.

## 02 — THE RIO RUGBY WEEKEND
Purpose: sell the trip.

Core idea:

**A weekend packed with quality rugby — and a full day at the beach in Rio with 15 of your best friends.**

Show:
- friends / team camaraderie;
- beach;
- Rio;
- rugby;
- post-match emotion;
- summer.

This section should make the visitor imagine the trip before discussing detailed tournament mechanics.

## 03 — THE TOURNAMENT
Purpose: prove sporting credibility without losing the festive character.

Communicate:
- quality competitive rugby;
- festive atmosphere;
- Beach Fives;
- men's + women's;
- 5v5;
- no rucks;
- no kicking in open play;
- no traditional set-piece formations;
- unlimited substitutions.

Visual treatment should feel more athletic and structured than the previous section, but still sunny and energetic.

## 04 — SINCE 2006
Purpose: trust and international proof.

Use:
- “Since 2006”;
- 20+ years;
- past international participation;
- country names or flags used tastefully.

Avoid a museum/timeline feel.

## 05 — WHY BRING YOUR TEAM?
Purpose: answer the manager/captain's decision question.

Use three pillars, in this order:

### THE EXPERIENCE
A memorable summer rugby weekend in Rio with your teammates.

### THE RUGBY
Fast, competitive Beach Fives against teams from Brazil and abroad.

### THE CITY
One of the world's most recognizable destinations as the setting for the weekend.

Do not repeat “history” here; heritage has already proven solidity in the previous section.

## 06 — SANDY SUMMER
Purpose: acquisition, brand awareness and sharing.

This is the visual transition into the arcade sub-brand.

Use:
- **PLAY SANDY SUMMER**
- **CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS**

The website can shift briefly into a contemporary 1990s arcade feel in this module.

This visual language must remain contained to:
- the Sandy Summer promotional section;
- the `/play` experience;
- share/result assets associated with the game.

Do not allow pixel-art/arcade styling to take over the main tournament website.

## 07 — THIS IS RIO BEACH RUGBY
Purpose: emotional proof through real imagery.

Use a curated editorial mood board of photos + short videos from past editions.

Recommended desktop mix:
- **8–10 photographs**
- **2 short video loops or clips**
- **10–12 total visual tiles**

Recommended mobile:
- show a tighter edit of approximately **6 photos + 1 short video** before the Instagram CTA;
- optionally allow horizontal swipe/carousel for additional media.

Priority image categories:
1. explosive rugby action: sidestep, tackle, dive, pass, sand spray;
2. strong men's competition;
3. strong women's competition;
4. team celebration / huddle / friendship;
5. recognizable Rio context: sea, mountains, Christ the Redeemer, Sugarloaf when naturally available;
6. wide beach/event atmosphere;
7. human close-up: smiles, fatigue, joy, intensity;
8. international cues: visiting team kit, flags or mixed teams;
9. one or two images that feel like “the best weekend with your team”, not only competition.

Gender representation should feel balanced across the gallery.

Avoid:
- repetitive action shots;
- 12 images of the same match angle;
- photos dominated by old Copacabana-specific pavement/signage;
- weak generic crowd photos;
- low-resolution archival images unless intentionally used in the heritage section.

End with:
**SEE MORE ON INSTAGRAM →**

# 12. GRAPHIC SYSTEM — HOW TO IMPLEMENT THE LOGO LANGUAGE

Create a reusable SVG asset file such as:

`rbr27-brand-motifs.svg`

It should contain three core motifs derived from the 2027 logo:

## Motif A — THE SUN ARC
Derived from the orange sweep around the logo.

Use for:
- section transitions;
- image crop edges;
- underlines;
- CTA motion;
- large partial arcs entering from the edge of the viewport.

## Motif B — THE RIO WAVE
Derived from the cobalt/blue splash and wave shapes.

Use for:
- masks around photography;
- subtle corner crops;
- video frames;
- occasional section separators;
- hover/motion detail.

## Motif C — THE SAND CUT
Derived from the logo's white / negative-space separation between orange and blue forms.

Use for:
- warm-cream negative spaces;
- asymmetric photo framing;
- section overlaps;
- subtle cut-through compositions.

Design rule:

**One strong branded gesture is better than five small decorative gestures.**

Maximum recommended frequency:
- approximately one major motif per viewport;
- never allow motifs to interfere with faces, ball action, CTAs or body text.

Motion:
- slow, restrained parallax or reveal is acceptable;
- no constant bouncing/wobbling;
- motion should feel editorial and premium.

Buttons:
- high contrast;
- large touch targets;
- preferably orange primary CTA on light/navy surfaces;
- short, action-oriented labels;
- minimal roundedness.

Cards:
- use sparingly;
- prefer editorial composition over grids of boxed cards.

Corners:
- mostly square or lightly softened.

Navigation:
- clean;
- compact;
- persistent enough to support conversion;
- “Bring Your Team” visually distinct.

Sections:
- alternate full-bleed photography, warm-light surfaces and occasional navy contrast;
- avoid every section being the same two-column template.

Before approving any page or concept, answer:
1. Does Rio read instantly?
2. Does the page make a rugby team want to travel?
3. Is the experience sold before administrative detail?
4. Does the rugby still feel credible and competitive?
5. Does the page feel festive without becoming a party flyer?
6. Are men's and women's rugby both visible?
7. Is photography doing more work than decoration?
8. Is the layout light and sunlit rather than dark/heavy?
9. Is orange used with restraint?
10. Is the 2027 logo unchanged?
11. Are logo-derived motifs controlled and reusable?
12. Is the Sandy Summer arcade language contained to its sub-brand?
13. Can a visitor reach WhatsApp quickly?
14. Is the copy concise and international?
15. Could the design belong specifically to Rio Beach Rugby rather than any beach tournament?
